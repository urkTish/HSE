# Comparators: "like Clonlara" and the "Hand in Hand" question

Agent 03 · researched 2026-10-30 · Phase 1.

## What Clonlara is (the reference point)

From Clonlara School's official site (clonlara.org, read 2026-10-30):

- A school founded in Ann Arbor, Michigan, with a **campus programme** there and
  an **Off-Campus Program** for families who "can live anywhere in the world and
  travel while enrolled"; it says it serves families in more than 80 countries.
- **Learner-centred:** families build an education plan with an assigned
  advisor (an experienced educator); parents oversee learning in K–8; high
  school students follow their interests and submit a credit request for each
  course at the end of each semester.
- **Accredited:** the site lists Accreditation International and the National
  Council for Private School Accreditation.
- **Diploma:** off-campus high-school students earn credits toward a Clonlara
  diploma.
- **Cost:** 2026–27 off-campus tuition K–8 $1,460 (new) / $1,250 (re-enrolling);
  Grades 9–12 $1,960 / $1,750 (currency not stated on the page; the school is
  U.S.-based).

### "Comparable to Clonlara" as attributes

A Canadian institution is comparable to the degree it has all five:

| # | Attribute | Test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognised status | Registered/classified by a province, or accredited, so its records and diploma count |
| 2 | Learner-directed programme | The learner's interests shape the plan; assessment by narrative/portfolio rather than tests alone |
| 3 | Distance support | A certified teacher or advisor supports the family wherever it lives |
| 4 | Recognised secondary credential | Grants a provincial diploma or credits toward one |
| 5 | Flexibility of setting | Home, travel or blended attendance allowed |

A Canadian subtlety: the provincial programmes that most resemble Clonlara are
**funded for residents of that province**, which ties a family to the province;
Clonlara itself does not.

## The "Hand in Hand" ambiguity

The v1.0 brief names "Hand in Hand" as a comparator with no location. Web
searches on 2026-10-30 found **no Canadian school of that name** and several
unrelated organisations abroad:

| Candidate | What it is | Fit with the brief |
|---|---|---|
| Hand in Hand: Center for Jewish-Arab Education in Israel | Network of state-recognised bilingual Hebrew–Arabic schools in Israel (ages 3–18) | A real, well-known "Hand in Hand" school network, but bilingual/integrated public-funded schools, not distance or learner-directed |
| Hand in Hand Christian Montessori (Minnesota, U.S.) | Christian Montessori preschool and a 3-day "homeschool academy" for K–12 (Roseville, Wayzata, Bloomington) | Matches "homeschool support + Montessori", local only, faith-based |
| Hand in Hand Parenting (California nonprofit, founded 1989) | Parenting education, not a school | Matches only if the user meant a parenting philosophy |
| Hand in Hand Options (Diocese of Nashville) | Support programme for students with learning differences | Weak |
| A "Hand in Hand" child-care listing (Yellow Pages, B.C./N.S.) | Child care, location unclear | Weak |

Until the user answers (open question 9), Phase 1 reads "Hand in Hand" as
"a flexible, learner-led, accredited programme that supports families at a
distance", i.e. the same attributes as Clonlara above.

**Conclusion for the coordinator.** If a Clonlara-like, learner-led, distance
programme with a recognised diploma is essential, **British Columbia** is the
strongest province (funded independent online learning schools with Dogwood
pathways), followed by **Alberta** (supervised home education run by private
authorities, with funding). Families can also keep Clonlara itself while living
in Canada, but whether a province accepts it as satisfying compulsory-attendance
rules differs: in B.C. a Clonlara student would normally also be registered as
a homeschooler; in Ontario a notification letter suffices; in Québec the
home-schooling regulation's notice, learning project and evaluation apply. This
is a reading of the rules above, not a confirmed ministry position; ask each
ministry in Phase 2.
