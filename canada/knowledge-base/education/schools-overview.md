### Reading the evaluation

- **Learner-directed and recognised.** Banbury (Calgary) is the only
  qualifying school combining a self-directed model with a full JK–12 range.
  The strongest learner-directed options with distance delivery — SelfDesign
  and Oak and Orca (B.C.) — are held back only by unpublished or undated fees
  (see `comparators.md`).
- **Waldorf** is the most widely available model with continuity: B.C. Group 1
  funding makes it markedly cheaper in B.C. (Nelson $6,240) than in Ontario
  ($22,500–$26,470). Only the Toronto Waldorf School is confirmed to run to
  Grade 12 with a diploma; Whistler Waldorf appears to (register) but is
  unverified.
- **Montessori beyond early childhood** is confirmed at Westmont (to Grade 8,
  then a project-based high school), Selkirk (to Grade 8) and The Element
  (Grades 7–12). Lauremont's Montessori stops at Grade 6.
- **Cost bands** (base tuition, one child): under $10k — Nelson; $10–15k —
  Selkirk, Calgary Waldorf, lower Westmont grades; $15–25k — Vancouver Waldorf,
  Banbury, The Element, Waldorf Academy, Westmont high school; over $25k —
  Toronto Waldorf high school, Mabin, Class Afloat.
- **Geography.** Qualifying schools cluster around Victoria, Vancouver/North
  Shore, Calgary, Toronto/York Region and Ottawa, plus Nelson (B.C. interior) and
  Lunenburg (N.S.). No qualifying school was confirmed in Québec in Phase 1,
  because the two Québec candidates do not publish gross tuition.

### Limits of this comparison

Phase 1 covered 26 institutions, not the full market (Ontario's private-school
list alone has about 1,640 rows; B.C. about 325 independent schools). Class
sizes, outcomes and teacher qualifications are mostly unpublished. No school was
contacted. Distances to cities (`nearby_cities_km`) are left for Phase 2, when
Agent 04's city records exist.
