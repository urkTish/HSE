Mobile layout at 360 px

What this decides. The mobile layout at 360 px: full-bleed map, search pill, layers in a full-screen sheet, profile in a three-snap bottom sheet, comparison transposed.

  • All targets are at least 44 px; the sheet handle is a button that cycles snaps (peek 148 px, half, 92%), so dragging is never required.
  • Profile keeps the desktop hierarchy; the climate chart re-flows to 328 px with single-letter months only below 26 px per column.
  • Comparison becomes one card per criterion with a row per place (numbered 1–3), because four columns cannot fit 360 px legibly.
  • Each phone frame is exactly 360 px wide; on a 360 px screen the frames stack.
