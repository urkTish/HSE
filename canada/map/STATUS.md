## Known limitations

- Boundaries are Natural Earth 1:50m (generalised, not legal); city points
  are representative 1:10m points (offsets up to a few km).
- Eight principal cities (Abbotsford, Barrie, Hamilton, Kitchener, Oshawa,
  Sarnia, Windsor, Trois-Rivières) have no
  linked climate record yet; their profile says so.
- City names are Natural Earth's (e.g. "Québec" for Quebec City); `name_fr`
  is null on the city records.
- The relief layer is small-scale imagery (z ≤ 6, shown to z9), not an
  elevation dataset.
- Agent 04's `attr` records are split into natural vs tourism layers by a
  `subcategory` word pattern; records with other words land in "Tourism".
- No region polygons: records carry points only.
- The dark base style ("Dark") is unmodified upstream and less polished than
  Positron (OpenFreeMap's own note).
- No live data of any kind; nothing in the app claims to be live.
- Tested in headless Chromium only (no Firefox/Safari, no physical phone).

## Proposed decisions (for the coordinator to enter in DECISIONS.md)

- **P-05-1 — Base map.** OpenFreeMap Positron (light) and Dark (dark) styles,
  keyless, with a built-in no-network fallback style; no third-party demo
  servers as fallback. Within D-005.
- **P-05-2 — Phase 1 boundaries and cities from Natural Earth v5.1.2**
  (permitted first pass); replaced by Statistics Canada in Phase 2 with stable
  IDs. City records are labelled `official_fact` as instructed, although
  Natural Earth is a cartographic, not a government, source; alternative:
  `professional_interpretation` — the coordinator may prefer that.
- **P-05-3 — What never enters static files.** Forecasts; `re` values and
  year-over-year changes; school tuition amounts; numeric price/rent/cost/fee
  fields in any category except `nat`; dollar amounts written in text (and
  percentages in `re` text). `nat` records keep legislated parameters (tax
  brackets, credits, programme fees). This is stricter than SPEC §9 B, which
  lists "tuition (with year)" in the education profile: the school year is
  shown, the amount comes on demand in Phase 4. Combined with D-008.
- **P-05-4 — Build tolerance.** `build-data` excludes records that fail
  validation (listed in `index.json`) and fails only when Agent 05's own lane
  fails; `--strict` fails on any failure, for the coordinator's pre-commit
  run. Alternative: fail on any failure (one lane could block the map).
- **P-05-5 — Generated data not committed.** `app/public/data/` is
  git-ignored and regenerated by `dev`, `build` and `build-data`; only the
  273 KB boundary file in `app/data-src/` and the `places` records are
  committed.
- **P-05-6 — Toolchain versions.** npm resolved the current majors on
  2026-10-30: Vite 8, TypeScript 6, ESLint 10, MapLibre GL JS 6, React 19,
  Tailwind 4 (lockfile committed). MapLibre 6 needs its worker URL registered
  explicitly (`setWorkerUrl` with a `?url` import).
